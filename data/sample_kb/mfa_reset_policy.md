# MFA Device Reset Policy

## When to Reset MFA
Employees who replace their smartphone or lose access to Okta Verify must request an MFA factor reset before signing back into corporate apps.

## Reset Procedure
1. Do not share passwords, recovery codes, or temporary tokens over Slack or email.
2. Submit an **MFA Reset Request** through the IT Service Desk or call the IT Helpdesk verification line.
3. A support technician will verify your identity via live video call with your employee badge or manager approval.
4. Once cleared, scan the new QR code in the Okta Verify app within 10 minutes.

## SLA & Priority
- Standard MFA resets are handled as **P3** tickets (target response: 2 business hours).
- Production on-call engineers locked out during an incident qualify for **P1** escalation.
